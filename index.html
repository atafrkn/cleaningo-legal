Cleaningo

Clean your gallery the fun way.

Cleaningo helps you review, organize and delete photos, videos, contacts and files on your iPhone or iPad. Swipe through your library card by card, find duplicates, similar bursts, blurry and broken shots, shrink large videos, and tidy up duplicate contacts.

Everything runs on your device — Cleaningo has no servers of its own, and your photos, contacts and files never leave your phone.

Nothing is deleted by accident: every removal goes to an in-app trash first, emptying it is double-confirmed, and photos stay in iOS "Recently Deleted" for 30 days.

Cleaningo is distributed on the Apple App Store for iOS.
